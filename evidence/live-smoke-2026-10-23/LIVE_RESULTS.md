## Caveats

- A single run: no repetition, so no between-run variance is reported.
- Workload: synthetic tickets, template labels. Template labels are the template author's, not independent ground truth; they are independent of the teacher only in that the teacher never wrote them.
- The hard templates are deliberately ambiguous: read their accuracy apart from the easy ones.
- Latency was measured at concurrency 4 from the harness host; client times include the gateway and the network.
- Whether Token Factory caches prompts is unverified; the control arm randomises call order and recorded 0 cached input tokens.
- Every dollar figure uses list prices, unverified until the day-1 checks pass (RATCHET_PRICES_VERIFIED=0).
- The measured all-teacher baseline is a ratio estimate from the control sample with a normal-approximation interval.
- A smoke run: it never backs a number in the docs.
