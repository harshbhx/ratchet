What moves to your adapter, and why

Scout's agents are plain OpenAI clients pointed at Ratchet. Each call carries a task name, and Ratchet routes by task.

  • Triage and Reader make many short calls with a fixed answer shape (a few labels and one fact). Ratchet logs Ultra's answers to them, trains a small Nemotron Nano model on those answers each night, and lets it answer when it is confident. When it is not, the call is escalated to Ultra.
  • Planner and Writer are open-ended. They are pinned to Ultra, are never used as training data, and never move.

The cost line on each step compares what the call cost with what it would have cost on Ultra alone. To watch Night School train new versions and release them (shadow, canary, rollback), open the dashboard.
