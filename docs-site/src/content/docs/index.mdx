## The loop at a glance

<BigPicture />

Three loops run at different speeds:

- **Every request** (milliseconds to seconds): the student samples its answer three times. If the samples agree and the confidence score reaches the version's threshold, the student's answer is served. If not, the teacher answers. A few served answers are audited by the teacher.
- **Every few minutes**: the release gate looks at the audits and moves a new version forward (shadow, then 10%, 50% and 100% of traffic) or rolls it back.
- **Every night**: Night School trains a fresh adapter on the teacher's answers only, tests it on a held-out golden set, and hands it to the release gate.

<Aside type="caution" title="What is real today">
The loop is built and tested offline, with mock models in a simulator. Simulation numbers in these docs come from mock models and are labelled. As of Oct 21, 2026, live mode, the GPU endpoint, the training job and the demo VM kit are written and tested against fakes, but have not run on a real Nebius account. Live numbers will come from a measured live run.
</Aside>

## Why "Ratchet"

A ratchet turns only one way. Every night more of your traffic moves from the model you rent to the adapter you own, and the gates are the pawl that stops it from slipping back: a bad release is rolled back automatically.
